# Neverland Lab Experiments

Riddle Team

## The band

In a Neverland lab, Dr. Fizzleworth, Prof. McGigglepants, and Madame Whiskerstein were testing a 'joy serum' when a fart in the fume hood caused a chain reaction. The lab exploded, but the serum turned their instruments into sentient, high-energy devices. Their first rehearsal was a disaster—Prof. McGigglepants set his pants on fire, Dr. Fizzleworth’s marimba played itself, and Madame Whiskerstein’s bacteria conducted the band. They formed Riddle Team to turn their chaos into music, blending science, games, and mischief into their signature sound.

**Dr. Bumblebee Fizzleworth** — Test-tube marimba. Always wears lab goggles, even on stage

**Professor Wobble McGigglepants** — Bunsen burner bass. Trips over his own feet during solos

**Madame Whiskerstein** — Petri-dish percussion. Talks to her bacteria during breaks

## Neverland Lab Experiments Tour

Fictional shows in the imaginary country Neverland.

- Day 1 · Fizzleburg · The Exploding Beaker — Dr. Fizzleworth's marimba plays a solo.
- Day 2 · Wobbleton · The Bunsen Burner Bar — Prof. McGigglepants trips during his solo.
- Day 3 · Whiskerville · The Petri Dish Palace — Madame Whiskerstein's bacteria conduct the band.
- Day 4 · Giggleport · The Test Tube Theater — The lab equipment starts playing itself.
- Day 5 · Fumehood · The Chaos Café — A fart in the fume hood causes a chain reaction.
- Day 6 · Riddleville · The Mad Scientist's Lounge — The joy serum turns the crowd into dancers.
